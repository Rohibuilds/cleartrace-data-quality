What ClearTrace provides

Deterministic checks identify missing values, duplicates, whitespace, category inconsistencies, invalid dates, non-numeric values, and statistical outliers. Findings show affected fields, sample locations, severity, and a plain-language recommendation. Candidate repairs can be edited, approved, or rejected before mutation. Revisions, comparisons, undo history, and audit records make accepted changes reviewable.
